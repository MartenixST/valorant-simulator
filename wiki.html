How to Play

Welcome to Valorant Career Simulator! Your goal is to build a dynasty by managing players, finances, and match strategies.

01. Create

Customize your player avatar and allocate initial stat points to define your playstyle.

02. Train

Use the Training Center daily to improve Aim, Game Sense, and Utility stats.

03. Recruit

Scout free agents and sign contracts. Balance your budget and roster synergy.

04. Compete

Enter tournaments and custom matches to earn XP, money, and fame.

Game Systems

Player Attributes

Aim

Determines raw mechanical skill, headshot %, and duel win rate.

Game Sense

Affects positioning, clutch ability, and reading enemy plays.

Utility

Effectiveness of ability usage, support score, and team impact.

Team Dynamics

Building a "Superteam" isn't just about stacking high stats. Synergy is calculated based on:

  • ✓ Shared nationality or region
  • ✓ Time played together on the roster
  • ✓ Role compatibility (e.g., Duelist + Initiator)
